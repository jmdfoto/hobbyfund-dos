# 10 — Mobile Polish

Spec section 5 & 8.

## Behavior
- Mobile-first, single column, large legible numbers — usable one-handed on a phone.
- Editable child name in the header (nice-to-have per spec, not critical — implement if time allows).
- Plain language throughout, appropriate for a child reader.

## Priority order (spec section 8, do not violate)
1. Ledger math correct, validation rules enforced.
2. Data survives a restart.
3. Comfortable one-handed phone use.
4. Visual polish.

## Acceptance Criteria
- App is usable and legible at a 375px-wide viewport with no horizontal scrolling.
- Tap targets for the four main actions are large enough for comfortable phone use.
- Child name is editable and persists (if implemented).
